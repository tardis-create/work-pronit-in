Trajectory

Operating stance

Quietly, precisely, without a marketing team.

No keynote circuit. No LinkedIn flex. The proof is in the repos, the deployed products, and the leverage per engineer.

I’ve spent a decade building things that outlive the current model. The first 50% of my career was research and infrastructure plumbing; the second 50% is shipping the leverage layer for India’s AI decade.

I optimise for trajectory, not income. Cash is fuel. The 10-year arc is the destination. Anything that pulls me off that arc — hype cycles, raise-timing pressure, follower counts — is a tax I refuse to pay.

My team is small and stays small. 5–12 elite operators outperforming 200 average ones is the only operating model I’ve seen scale without diluting the work.

Privacy isn’t a feature I add later. Privacy is architecture. The data path is the product. If it leaves the box, the design is wrong.

Don’t build apps. Build infrastructure. The next decade of India is built on the leverage layer — not the surface apps.
— Pronit Das

Operating principles

6 things I won’t compromise on.

Not values for a wall. Operating constraints that shape what I build, who I work with, and what I say no to.

01

Infrastructure over applications

If it can’t outlive the current model, it isn’t a product. It’s a demo with a billing plan.

02

Leverage over headcount

5–12 elite operators outperform 200 average ones. Every collaboration is judged on leverage-per-engineer.

03

Trajectory over income

I optimise for the 10-year arc, not the next 90 days. Cash is fuel, not a finish line.

04

Inevitability over attention

If the work is real, attention follows. If I need attention, the work isn’t real yet.

05

Privacy is architecture, not policy

Not a feature, not a checkbox, not a marketing line. The data path is the product.

06

India is the bet

1.4B people, 22 languages, the hardest geocoding problem on Earth. The next decade of infrastructure is built here.

FAQ

The questions I get asked most.

Long-form, unedited, and the same answer I’d give in person.

What does Pronit Das specialize in?
GeoAI, autonomous developer agents, and Cloudflare edge infrastructure for India. I’m the founder of TARDIS Solutions, where a 5-person team ships 7 live products and 100+ open-source projects. The through-line: build infrastructure, not apps.
Who founded TARDIS Solutions?
I did, in 2022. TARDIS is an AI-native infrastructure company focused on India-first GeoAI, autonomous developer agents, and EUDR compliance platforms. The team is fully remote, edge-native on Cloudflare, and small on purpose.
What is AUI (Autonomous Ultra Instinct)?
AUI is a local-LLM developer agent that lives in your terminal. It reads your codebase, writes code, runs CI, opens PRs — and never sends your code to the cloud unless you explicitly ship it. It’s the agent I wished I had a decade ago.
What is Dhruva?
Dhruva is an offline-first India geocoder built in Rust. It supports 22 scheduled languages, runs on low-end phones, and is designed for the field worker who needs an accurate address or a reverse geocode without connectivity.
Where is Pronit Das based?
India. TARDIS Solutions is registered in India and operates as a fully remote, edge-native team across Cloudflare’s global network. The HQ is wherever the best leverage is, this week.

Is Pronit Das hiring?
